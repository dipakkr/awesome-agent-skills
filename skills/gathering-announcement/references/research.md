# Placement and creative research

Checked 2026-10-28.

LinkedIn Event creation guidance specifies a 16:9 cover and at least 480 pixels width. Event cover and Event Ad specifications are different; these examples target the Event cover.

Creative inference from LinkedIn B2B Institute research: build a distinctive visual around useful problem-led information, rather than a dense list of features. This is a design direction, not a prediction of engagement, virality or sales. No source artwork was copied.
