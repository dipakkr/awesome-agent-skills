# Why repair deserves a place

An original demonstration essay, Local repair as shared knowledge. Not a published study.

Repair can be a shared practice, not just a purchase decision. A local repair table makes practical knowledge easier to exchange.

The essay offers observations, not a measured environmental impact study.

A practical way to explore this idea is to choose one safe class of everyday objects. Ask experienced volunteers what they can support. Record questions people bring, not just items repaired.
