# Four worked examples

Each output changes the subject and content, not only the theme. These are authored examples, not an independent customer or agent reuse test. Full recorded MP4s and posters are in `examples/recorded/`.

## 1. Two paths through checkout

Use $data-pan-animation to make "Two paths through checkout" for the fictional brand Cartnote. Use examples/01.json as the exact input, the paper palette and the CTA "Review checkout". Render the complete silent film, poster and editable inputs locally. Keep the supplied illustrative disclosure. Use my supplied continuous clip, at least 6.4 seconds long, and do not loop it.

```sh
python3 <installed-skill>/scripts/render.py <installed-skill>/examples/01.json --out output/example-01 --presenter /path/to/your-continuous-clip.mp4
```

Inputs: [examples/01.json](../examples/01.json). Recorded film: [film.mp4](../examples/recorded/01/film.mp4).
