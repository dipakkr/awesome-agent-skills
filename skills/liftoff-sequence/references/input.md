# Editable brief

`mode`: `rocket`. `format`: landscape (1920x1080), portrait (1080x1920), square (1080x1080), all30fps. `brand`:1-20 characters; `headline`:1-42; `ending`:1-45; `cta`:1-30; `url`:1-32; `chapters`:exactly four strings of1-38 characters; `accent`:six-digit hex; `subject`:capsule or satellite; `concept`:true. ASCII/Latin examples use the bundled fonts; other scripts need an appropriate licensed font and fit review.

Text, accent and the documented subject branches are direct inputs. Different stories, subjects and shot durations require editing the original `src/Rocket.jsx` plus timeline metadata in `src/model.mjs`. Rendering runs locally. Examples do not claim that this fixed starting template can automatically make every possible film.
