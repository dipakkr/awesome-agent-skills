# Direction

Use technical line drawings on white before the black flight stage. The same original vehicle must persist through exploded assembly, countdown, ignition and tracking flight. Metallic gradient sections, structural seams, payload details and exhaust make the vehicle tangible. Satellite and capsule use different deployment geometry. Use engineered labels and sparse typography; do not imply telemetry represents a real launch. Sound has low pulses, three countdown tones, ignition noise and a release chord.

Inspect side by side with another skill. The main subject, material, framing, type, action and sound should be recognizably different even before the title is read.
