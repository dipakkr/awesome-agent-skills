# Output and placement notes

Working defaults, not universal platform acceptance rules: a 4:5 feed image at 1440 x 1800, a square adaptation at 1440 x 1440 when requested, and a separately composed 9:16 vertical master at 1080 x 1920. Request the actual destination and locale when it changes the deliverable. Measure exported pixels; do not relabel a mismatched render. Recompose or edit with the available image tool rather than stretching a finished image.

For a short motion ad, a 6-12 second concept is a starting point, not a mandatory duration. Use actual footage or create intentional product/camera motion with an available video tool. Keep identity stable. Export H.264 MP4/yuv420p for a broad-compatible review master. A GIF is a preview or destination-specific asset, not the default Reels/TikTok upload. Include licensed or original audio where the placement needs it; silent motion is not automatically a publish-ready TikTok ad.

Keep copy away from UI overlays and inspect the real placement preview. No single pixel safe-zone works for all captions, anchors and devices. TikTok's June 2026 in-feed specification recommends vertical 9:16 and specifies at least 540 x 960 for that format. Its downloadable safe zones vary by format, caption, anchor and language:
https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads?lang=en

The April 2026 format/functionality policy separately discusses motion, audio and destination requirements. Specification and policy pages can differ in scope; confirm the chosen ad format in Ads Manager rather than apply one global duration rule:
https://ads.us.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-ad-format-and-functionality
