# Source and asset notes

Claude Directory authored the renderer, instructions, sample briefs and geometric characters. User-provided motion archives informed high-level layout and timing ideas. Their source code, logos, music, characters and stock media are not bundled in this original package. This is an independent implementation; it is not affiliated with the reference creators.

DM Sans and DM Serif Display are bundled under their accompanying SIL Open Font License files. Python/Pillow/FFmpeg are external runtime dependencies with their own licenses.

Presenter demos, where present, use the previously acquired Alena Darmel Pexels clip9032610. They are labelled stock, make no endorsement claim and contain original graphic context. The standalone stock source is excluded. Supply your own continuous footage to reproduce the effect.

The media-wall samples reuse original AI-generated artwork already authored for Claude Directory's slide collection. They are concept images, not real products, places or customer work. No new image or video generation service was called for this collection. Asset lineage is recorded in the local collection documentation.
