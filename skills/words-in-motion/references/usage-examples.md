# Four worked examples

Each output changes the subject and content, not only the theme. These are authored examples, not an independent customer or agent reuse test. Full recorded MP4s and posters are in `examples/recorded/`.

## 1. Launch with clarity

Use $words-in-motion to make "Launch with clarity" for the fictional brand Cartnote. Use examples/01.json as the exact input, the paper palette and the CTA "Build your store brief". Render the complete silent film, poster and editable inputs locally. Keep the supplied illustrative disclosure.

```sh
python3 <installed-skill>/scripts/render.py <installed-skill>/examples/01.json --out output/example-01
```

Inputs: [examples/01.json](../examples/01.json). Recorded film: [film.mp4](../examples/recorded/01/film.mp4).
