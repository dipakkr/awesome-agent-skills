# Shopify image guidance

Checked 2026-10-28. Shopify recommends 2048 × 2048 for square product images and consistent ratios across featured products. Product help lists a 25MP/5000px ceiling, while theme/API guidance lists 20 MP; conservatively stay below 20 MP and 20 MB and verify the actual upload route. Banner crop depends on section/theme; there is no universal collection-banner dimension. Use focal points where supported and check mobile in the destination theme. Generated output dimensions must be measured; a smaller render is not a compliant 2048px master.

- Shopify product media types: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/product-media-types
- Shopify theme images and focal points: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/images/theme-images
- Shopify developer media limits: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/product-merchandising/products-and-collections/manage-media
