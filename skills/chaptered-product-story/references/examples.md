# Four complete examples

These are authored fictional product films, not live integrations or independent customer tests. Each changes its subject, copy and three original interface assets. All run for 40 seconds at 1080p/30fps and use the bundled original instrumental.

1. **Fieldnote**: a research workspace moving from source notes through a recommendation to a handoff. Ink and warm gold.
2. **Framehouse**: a design studio moving from references through an editorial canvas to a focused review. Paper and sage.
3. **Lessonroom**: an educator's tool moving from learning goal through example to practice. Plum and lavender.
4. **Trailcase**: a fictional travel planner moving from saved places through an itinerary to pre-trip checks. Forest and pale green. No bookings or real destination claims.

For example 01:

```sh
python3 <installed-skill>/scripts/render.py <installed-skill>/examples/01/brief.json --out output/fieldnote
```

Use 02, 03 or 04 for the others. Each example folder contains its brief, three source screens and `recorded/film.mp4`. To create a new product film, write a new brief and supply its own captures; do not merely rename the example brand.
