# Screen Showcase Card

Show a product idea through polished device and interface compositions with concise copy and believable visual hierarchy. Start with [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md) for a copyable first request. The examples demonstrate a visual method; their subjects, demo names and copy are not mandatory defaults.

## Art direction

1. Use supplied screenshots for a real product; inspect and preserve their content. Without them, mark the brief and handoff as a fictional interface concept.
2. Choose a hero surface and a small supporting fragment: a device, a tilted window, a stack or a card trail. Do not scatter many equal-weight screens.
3. Keep UI labels deliberately few and legible. Never invent customer counts, revenue, ratings or unsupported product capabilities.
4. Balance the screen and headline with material, angle and lighting. Raster UI concepts are not working software or editable component files.

## Adapt the brief

Collect the subject, audience, placement, exact headline, optional supporting line, palette and authorized reference assets. Short copy is easier to render and read. A logo is optional; omit it when absent rather than inventing a replacement. For a real product or person, inspect supplied references and preserve identity. Treat generated brands, people and interface screens as fictional concepts, not evidence about real customers or products.

Choose a different composition when the subject calls for it: change focal point, crop, type hierarchy and material interaction. Four requested variants should explore distinct concepts, not merely recolour one image. Read [references/examples.json](references/examples.json) for four authored briefs and [references/usage-examples.md](references/usage-examples.md) for their exact prompts and outputs.

## Generate and inspect

Use an available image-generation tool for actual raster artwork. Read [references/image-tools.md](references/image-tools.md) when selecting a model or handling references. No particular paid provider is required; the user's connected tool determines access and cost. A text-only agent can prepare the prompt but must say that generation still needs a tool.

Save the brief, exact prompt, actual tool/model information when exposed, native dimensions and output hash. Inspect the entire image and a thumbnail: check every visible word, margins, hierarchy, reference fidelity, anatomy/geometry and unsupported claims. Reject defects and make focused corrections with the image tool; retain earlier attempts separately. Do not relabel a crop or recolour as an independent concept.

Deliver the accepted image, alt text, editable brief, exact prompt and a short use note. These are generation inputs and raster images, not layered PSDs, editable 3D models or working UI code. Measure dimensions; do not infer that a requested size was delivered. For a specific OG/social placement, compose or adapt to its requested ratio and recheck cropping and safe areas before export. Keep publishing separate unless requested.

## Tested scope

Four independently generated fictional concepts, using built-in image_gen, were visually reviewed. The underlying model identifier was not exposed. Other providers, real-person/brand fidelity, exact downstream platform cropping and commercial performance have not been tested by these examples. Use your own facts and authorized assets for production work.
