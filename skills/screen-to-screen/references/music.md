# Music

Use a music file that the user can use for the intended publication. The renderer requires a credit text file whenever music is supplied, copies that credit into delivery and embeds it in MP4 metadata. It trims from --music-start (seconds), fades edges and normalizes to approximately -16LUFS. Supply the complete credit in the publishing description; embedded metadata alone is insufficient.

Recorded examples: Neon (Device Handoff) and Origami (Editorial Collage), by Scott Buckley. Commercial use with attribution under CC BY4.0. Raw MP3s are not bundled; obtain your own authorized copy or use another cleared track.

The included visual examples are silent if rendered without --music. Do not present a successful encode or loudness measurement as a subjective audio review.
