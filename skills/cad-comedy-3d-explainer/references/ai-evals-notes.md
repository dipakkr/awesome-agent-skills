# AI Evals example: factual basis

Reviewed October 29, 2026 against Anthropic's primary-source article [Demystifying evals for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents), especially evaluation structure, conversational agents and regression evaluations.

The skit explains evaluation as realistic test inputs plus explicit scoring criteria, with a fictional customer-support example. Its sequence is: inspect failures, improve the system, rerun comparable tests and broaden coverage with observed failures. Passing a finite set is not a universal reliability guarantee.

The self-awarded diploma is a joke about unchecked self-approval. It does not imply that AI cannot help author test cases or grade outputs. Automated, model-based and human grading can all be appropriate; the short reel does not teach their full methodology. No actual model benchmark, customer result or measured score is claimed.
