# Inputs and limits

Common fields: `brand` (24 characters), `title` (46), `subtitle` (88), `source` (85), and `theme` (chalk, ink, rose, cobalt). Text is Latin-script with a limited punctuation set; geometry measurement may reject copy that is within the character cap but does not fit. Do not silently truncate.

`people`: two labels, each up to 16 characters. `messages`: exactly four objects with `from` equal to 0 or 1 and `text` up to 60 characters, fitting two measured lines. `closing`: up to 35 characters. Entrances are 1, 4.2, 7.4 and 10.6 seconds in a 16-second film. The layout is landscape, with no scrolling or app impersonation.
