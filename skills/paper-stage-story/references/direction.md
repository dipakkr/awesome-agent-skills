# Direction

Use a white paper stage, serif chapter captions, warm ink, a tactile orange or pink courier and green supporting props. Layer shadows, grain and slightly irregular edges. Animate the bird’s legs and wings, move scenery at a different speed, use a rain obstacle, and show an actual consequence of delivery. Seed and letter branches have different hero objects and arrival actions. Keep the close readable and give delicate movement time to settle.

Inspect side by side with another skill. The main subject, material, framing, type, action and sound should be recognizably different even before the title is read.
